# Levels

**Status:** Draft (2026-11-25). The items below are **Agreed**; the room generator, placement of fountains and hazards, and the difficulty curve are not written yet.

## Spawners, not packs

**Agreed** (2026-11-25). Enemies roam the map from spawners; the rule and why: [enemies.md](enemies.md#no-groups).

- Running through the map aggroes many enemies and kills the slime.
- Luring them one at a time from the edge works.

## Room size and vision

**Agreed** (2026-11-25). Room size and enemy vision are difficulty levers ([vision.md](vision.md#death-and-difficulty)).

- **Short vision:** the slime must stand close to aggro two at once.
- **Long vision:** everyone sees the slime when a door opens, so the slime opens doors from the side and peeks.
- Walls block sight ([turn-structure.md](turn-structure.md)).

## Level size grows

**Agreed** (2026-11-25). Each level is bigger than the one before: cells per room, cells per level, rooms per level. The growth law comes from balance (pending).

**Working** (coordinator): a level takes at most 15 minutes (≤ 7 rooms), and levels grow in content and difficulty rather than length. Which of the two rules wins is open ([route.md](route.md#shape-of-a-run)).
