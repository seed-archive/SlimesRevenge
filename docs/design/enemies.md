# Enemies

**Status:** Draft (2026-11-25). The items below are **Agreed**; the roster, abilities and drops are not written yet. Today's roster in the code: [creatures.md](../creatures.md).

## No groups

**Agreed** (2026-11-25). There are no enemy groups or packs (the game is not XCOM). Enemies come one at a time: they roam the map from spawners and the player lures them one by one ([levels.md](levels.md)). Enemies met in a corridor count as singles.

## Fast enemies

**Agreed** (2026-11-25).

- A fast enemy may move and bite in one turn (a dash).
- Such enemies have low hit points, so the slime can kill one in a turn and step back.
- Their speed and hit points come from balance (pending).
