# Story

**Status:** Draft (2026-11-26). The twist (the slime takes revenge on the player) and the frame are **Agreed**; the origin is Oleg's direction; the ending is **Open** (lore options wait for Oleg's choice). Detail: the design notes `lore/origin.md`, `lore/fourth-wall.md`, `lore/endings-options.md`, `lore/hunters.md`.

## Frame

**Agreed.**

- Everyone has wronged the slime: in every game the weakest monster is a rat or a slime.
- The goal: get through to the alchemist. The slime also wants to see the world.
- The meeting turns "Luke, I am your father" around; "Hello, dad" is out. **Working** (lore): "No… I am your son".
- The alchemist need not be a slime: he can drink any liquid and turn into anything. The "the alchemist was a slime himself" twist is out.
- The game stays for children: no drugs.

## Origin

**Direction** (Oleg): the alchemist was building a golem. The part that should have given the golem a soul rolled down the drain, reached the quarry, became aware of itself and became the slime. **The golem is the slime's brother.** Lore builds the hints and endings on this (a cracked vial next to the slime at the start; the golem's empty chest socket).

## The twist: revenge on the player

**Agreed** (2026-11-25): the player is the one who killed slimes in other games. Everyone thinks the slime avenges itself on the alchemist; in fact it avenges itself on the player. This is the main twist.

- **Agreed:** no fight in which the player defends the interface. After the alchemist falls, the slime knocks on the interface, breaks the glass and flows out onto the player's screen — the hook for a second game.
- **Agreed:** checking which other games the player owns is dropped; the game never looks at other apps.
- **Agreed (direction):** mid-game a notification blinks and opens itself: "you have a letter", a letter from the alchemist. Details are lore's.
- **Agreed:** the letters do not tell the player about the chase. The order to catch the slime is addressed to the alchemist's squad and found on it, so the player works out who sent the killers.
- Past slimes of the player lie as puddles where they died ([progression.md](progression.md#past-slimes)).
- **Open:** how the game reaches the player outside itself (a real push after the finale, an exit notification on PC). App Store, Google Play and Steam rules must be checked before any of it is built.

## The alchemist and the finale

**Agreed.**

- The alchemist summons his masterpiece, the golem. Two phases: the golem first, then the alchemist. They hit with different things, so the slime needs two sets.
- The golem takes **2 units from the edge** per blow, so valuables belong in the middle of the body, not the deepest centre ([volume.md](volume.md#body-order)).
- On Easy and Normal the alchemist says he did not know he had a son. On Hard the slime answers "but you sent your killers after me" — a Hard-only ending.
- The golem can turn against its maker ([curios.md](curios.md#the-golem-turn)).
- **Open:** what happens at the meeting, and which of lore's endings (A "made on purpose", B "the son is the golem", C "the slime is you", D, E) Oleg picks.

## Hunters and the alchemist's squad

**Agreed.**

- Hunters appear **only on Hard**, at most once per level: locals (guards, assassins, dwarf berserkers) who try to kill the slime and drop lines ("finally we found you").
- **Direction** (Oleg): they look for the soul essence the alchemist wants back. He first reached into the drain himself, then sent a squad downstream as far as the quarry.
- **The squad by difficulty:** Easy — the slime finds the squad's remains and can eat them; Normal — finds them but cannot eat them; Hard — finds the squad alive and must beat it. On Hard the squad shouts "It's alive!" and tries to kill or catch the slime.
- **Working** (balance, `balance/hunters.md`, `balance/squad.md`): hunters from map 2, one per Hard level except guardian levels; the squad replaces the hunter on level 3; numbers there.
- **Open:** a defeat by the squad as the ending "In the jar" instead of death (lore proposal).
