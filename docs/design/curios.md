# Curios and single units

**Status:** Draft (2026-11-26). The purpose of curios, the ale and the final pay-off by difficulty are **Agreed**; single units are Oleg's direction; the rest is **Open** (lore proposals). Detail: the design notes `lore/special-units.md`, `lore/single-units.md`, `balance/special-units.md`.

## What a curio is

**Agreed.**

- Festive, one-off, unique units the slime buys or gets at a stop. The game stays for children: no drugs.
- **The point:** the player carries a useless unit through half the game, where a useful liquid could be, and it pays off at the very end.
- **Direction** (Oleg, to lore), the next five points. **Single units** are carried one at a time and used or handed over; no build is made of them. Now: the dwarf ale and the Aphrodisiac.
- **Single units react with nothing**, so a unique unit cannot be lost by mistake.
- Single units and Mercury do not get along by design: singles lie deep in the body and make holding 60 % Mercury harder.
- An achievement for collecting every single unit; a set bonus maybe, carefully (later).
- **Not everything in one run:** fallback options became normal ones. At the start the run draws one option from a pool (the mine: dwarf smithy / dwarf pantry / chest / nothing; the grove: Pan or the Hive; the graveyard: the Strzyga or a vampire). All bosses, singles and secrets take 2–3 runs. **Working** (balance, `balance/variety.md`): an unseen option weighs twice as much in the draw.

## The list

- **Dwarf ale** (Agreed): the smith sells or gives a unit of ale "by accident". It can be traded at the apothecary or gives a unique bonus in the final fight; it is required for the final pay-off.
- **Honey** (Oleg's direction): one unit per game, from the Hive in the grove by the mill (map 2). The Hive does not attack; it releases bees of 1 HP; beat it, get the honey.
- **Aphrodisiac** (Agreed: the name stays): drops from the siren Ligeia. It can be applied to any living mob (to charm it), or carried to someone as a trade. Oleg earlier: bosses are not affected.
- **Open** (lore): undertaker's ink, smelling salts, amber (from Resin + Lava), a banshee's tear, mandrake sap, Strzyga blood.
- **Open:** how single units relate to curios. The Aphrodisiac is both a single unit and one of the 17 liquids (it is spat); the ale is both a single unit and a curio.
- **Working** (balance): at most 3 curios in the body; a curio is not a liquid (no strike, no puddle, no reaction, not in affinity), it is knocked out to the floor and picked up again, and it separates reacting units.

## The final pay-off

**Agreed** by difficulty (the rule).

- A player who carried the "useless" things long enough gets a decisive advantage in the final fight; there is practically no way to lose then. The price is hauling junk through half the game.
- **Easy:** the ale. **Normal:** the ale and an item from any secret place. **Hard:** the ale, an item from a secret place and the victory over the squad. The ale is required on every difficulty (bought at the first shop, carried all game).
- **Working** (balance): the set is carried from level 9; numbers in `balance/special-units.md`.
